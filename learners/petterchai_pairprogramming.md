# What is pair programming?
 Two people sharing a machine by definition, one is a navigator having a broader overview and the other is a driver keeping eye to technical details.

# How does a good pair behave?
 - Reflecting on role separation such as navigator maintaining a good strategey and driver focussing on technical details.
 - Planning ahead
 - Deciding to take breaks while working together.
 - Being transparent and communicate
 - Don't fall asleep.

# When to switch roles? Give a procedure
 - Be transparent with skills and prospective issues
 - Plan around objective and issues
 - Divide the project ahead in parts and plan who will be the navigator and driver for each part
 - TDD in pairs (ping-pong strategy)

# What effects does pair programming have?
 - shared responsibility
 - skill and knowledge transfer
 - human connection
