## Research question
Is Summer the season with the highest difference in temperature across time?

## Hypothesis
Temperature in Summer is hotter in recent yearly than before, compared to the other seasons.

## Methods
We test our hypothesis by first measuring the differences between temperatures at the start and end of the measurement period of every season. Since we believe that the greatest changes have occurred in the last 20 years (i.e. 2000-2022), we will compare this time frame with the same but from 200 years ago (i.e. 1800-1822), ignoring measurement before or in between.

We will compare the average difference in Summer in the selected time frame to the other three seasons, using a one-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov test. We use an alpha value of 0.05.
