Discussion about pair programming.

1) What is pair programming?
Pairs programming is 2 people writing code together in the same machine. Very close collaboration in planning, discussing, structuring code. 

2) How does a good pair behave? Describe what can be observed when pairing online.
Respectfully. Taking upond different roles: driver and navigator. The navigator is the guide, observer, looking at the broad picture, larger issues. The driver, on the other hand, focuses on the small task at hand, coding, very narrow perspective. 
A good  pair can/should switch roles. Discuss, agree on small tasks.

3) When to switch roles? Give a procedure
Agree upon in advance. 
When implementing TDD, pairs should change roles every TDD cycle.
Witch keyboard regularly, general rule every 25 min aprox. 

4) What effects does pair programming have?
Learn from each other. Share knowlendge.
Come up with better solutions if you work in pair. Leads to a better structure. Code reviewing while coding. Checking other's mistakes.
You allow for better concetration spans.
