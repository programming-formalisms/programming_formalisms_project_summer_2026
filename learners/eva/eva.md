Making a simple change

Do all months experience the same changes in temperature over time?

Registered report:

**Research question:** Do all months experience the same changes in temperature over time?

**Hypotheses:** The temperature change is the same for all months.

**Methods:**
We test our hypothesis by comparing the average monthly temperatures at the start and end of our measurement period. To prevent that the temperatures are too related, we compare the first third of the measurements (i.e. 1722-1822) with the last third of the measurements (i.e. 1922-2022) and ignoring the measurements in between. 

We compare these monthly average temperatures with a two-sided Kolmogorov-Smirnov test (so to avoid assuming an increase/decrease, nor a distribution on these average temperatures), with the null hypothesis that these distributions are identical. We use an alpha value of 0.05. If the measured p value is below the alpha, then we reject the null hypothesis that the distributions are identical and we conclude that the temperatures have changed.
