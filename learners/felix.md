## Research Question
Is there a mean temeperature difference between summer (Jun-Aug) and winter (Dec-Feb)?

## Hypotheses
The temperature will be different between the four seasons: colder in winter, warmer in summer.

## Methods
Use an unpaired, parametric statistical test to evaluate the null hypothesis that there is no difference in mean temperature between the summer and winter seasons in the dataset. A p-value below 0.05 rejects the null hypothesis. 

## Results
A boxplot comparing the mean temperatures of the summer and winter seasons. 

## Discussion
A correlation between season and mean temperature is described.
