## Research Question
Is there a change in temperature over time and does this change differ between seasons?

### Hypothesis
There is a larger change during the winter than the summer.

## Methods
A linear model plotting temperature with year as one predictor and season (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall) as the second predictor (as well as their interaction)

## Results
- A plot of temperature over time with separate lines for season
- P values of significance tests of the beta values of the model.
