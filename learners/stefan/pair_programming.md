What is pair programming?
To program together in pairs where you take turns coding and overseeing. 

How does a good pair behave? Describe what can be observed when pairing online
Take breaks and agree on a schedule beforehand. Plan the task a bit beforehand. The navigator should mainly keep focus on medium term problems. There can be different roles, like expert-expert, expert-novice, these vary in effeciency and how much learning is achieved. There should be a shared workspace.
When to switch roles? Give a procedure
Fixing the time (e.g. 5 minutes). Solve test, design test -> switch.
What effects does pair programming have?
Good for learning purposes and time consumption.
