What is pair programming?
It is a way of work in which two  programmers work together on a shared piece of code.

How does a good pair behave? Describe what can be observed when pairing online
A good pair can coordinate: no one is getting bored and together they decide how and when to switch. 
They share the whole screen, communicate actively and acknowledge and leverage each other expertise.

When to switch roles? Give a procedure
Switching could be task based, time/energy based, expertise based. 
If one of the participants is more experienced then the switch should be expertise based, 
in case the scheme is test-driven development the switch should be task based, in any other combination
switching should be time/energy based.

What effects does pair programming have?
Knowledge sharing, ongoing code review, two brains are better than one, collective code ownership. prom
